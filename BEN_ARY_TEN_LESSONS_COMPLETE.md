# UNIT 0 — PRELIMINARY INSTRUCTIONS & TYPEWRITER MECHANICS
*Anatomy, posture, home row seating & touch orientation* — **Pages 5-11**

### Overview
Touch typing is typing by touch rather than by sight. Every finger has a designated home position on the home row (second row from bottom). The home row is: Left hand A S D F, Right hand J K L ;. Thumbs control the Space bar. Position at the machine: sit erect directly facing the center of the typewriter, feet flat on the floor, elbows close to the body, wrists floating naturally without resting on the frame, and fingers curved like a pianist.

## Lesson 0.1: Definitions of Major Functional Parts

**Instructions & Finger Movements:**
Learn the core mechanics: 1. Keyboard (Letter & figure keys), 2. Space Bar (thumb operated), 3. Shift Keys & Shift Lock (for capitals & upper symbols), 4. Backspace, 5. Cylinder / Platen & Cylinder Knobs, 6. Carriage Release, 7. Line-Space Lever (for returning carriage and advancing lines), 8. Paper Guide & Margin Stops.

**Drill Practice Text:**
```text
asdf jkl; asdf jkl; fj dk sl a; asdf jkl; fj dk sl a; asdf jkl; fj dk sl a;
```

**Key Objectives:**
- Identify machine controls
- Ergonomic seating
- Fingers curved over home row
- **Mastery Benchmark:** Locate home keys by touch without looking

---

## Lesson 0.2: Finding Home Row by Touch

**Instructions & Finger Movements:**
Pass lightly over the space bar without pressing it down, let your fingers come to rest on the bottom row of keys, then move slowly up to the second row (home row). Without looking down, place: Left hand little finger on A, ring on S, middle on D, index on F; Right hand index on J, middle on K, ring on L, little finger on ;. Keep thumbs hovering over the space bar.

**Drill Practice Text:**
```text
a s d f j k l ; asdf jkl; asdf jkl; a s d f j k l ; asdf jkl;
```

**Key Objectives:**
- Blind tactile seating
- Independent finger placement
- Immediate return to home row
- **Mastery Benchmark:** 100% blind placement on home keys

# UNIT 1 — BASIC HORIZONTAL COMBINATIONS
*Home row foundations: a-s-d-f-g and ;-l-k-j-h, alternating & scrambled* — **Pages 12-16**

### Overview
The foundational exercise of the Basic Combinations Method. Strike each key with a crisp, staccato snap and return immediately to the home position. Follow the dictation rhythm.

## Lesson 1.1: Left Hand Horizontal Combination (a-s-d-f-g)

**Instructions & Finger Movements:**
With the little finger of the left hand, strike a. With the next finger, strike s. With the next finger, strike d. With the index finger, strike f. Now extend the 'f' finger to the spare key next to it, strike g, and snap back to f. Space with thumb.

**Drill Practice Text:**
```text
asdfg asdfg asdfg asdfg asdfg asdfg asdfg asdfg asdfg asdfg asdfg asdfg
```

**Key Objectives:**
- Sharp finger stroke
- Snap index finger back from g to f
- Hold steady rhythm
- **Mastery Benchmark:** 99% accuracy on left hand sweep

---

## Lesson 1.2: Right Hand Horizontal Combination (;-l-k-j-h)

**Instructions & Finger Movements:**
With the little finger of the right hand, strike ; (semi). With the next finger, strike l. With the next, strike k. With index finger, strike j. Extend the 'j' finger to the spare key next to it, strike h, and snap back to j. Space with thumb.

**Drill Practice Text:**
```text
;lkjh ;lkjh ;lkjh ;lkjh ;lkjh ;lkjh ;lkjh ;lkjh ;lkjh ;lkjh ;lkjh ;lkjh
```

**Key Objectives:**
- Right pinky precision on semicolon
- Snap index finger back from h to j
- Even cadence
- **Mastery Benchmark:** 99% accuracy on right hand sweep

---

## Lesson 1.3: Alternating Hands Combination (asdfg ;lkjh)

**Instructions & Finger Movements:**
Alternate left hand then right hand with a single space bar tap between groups, saying aloud: asdfg space ;lkjh space asdfg space ;lkjh.

**Drill Practice Text:**
```text
asdfg ;lkjh asdfg ;lkjh asdfg ;lkjh asdfg ;lkjh asdfg ;lkjh asdfg ;lkjh asdfg ;lkjh asdfg ;lkjh
```

**Key Objectives:**
- Hand-to-hand rhythm
- Thumb spacebar coordination
- Zero keyboard looking
- **Mastery Benchmark:** Continuous steady rhythm across 4 lines

---

## Lesson 1.4: Scrambled Combinations & Practice Model 1

**Instructions & Finger Movements:**
Scramble the dictation of letters across both combinations without looking at your paper. Follow stroke for stroke and space for space. Apply to genuine home row sentences.

**Drill Practice Text:**
```text
asdfg ;lkjh asdfg ;lkjh a lad has a glad dad; dad had half a shad salad; all lads fall as a glad lad falls; a flask has a fall; asks a lad; glass flask falls; flag has a fall
```

**Key Objectives:**
- No hesitation pauses

- 98%+ accuracy
- **Mastery Benchmark:** Type Practice Model 1 with 0 errors

# UNIT 2 — BASIC DIAGONAL COMBINATIONS
*Diagonal reaches: aqaz, swsx, dedc, frfv, gtgb, ;p;/, lol., kik,, jujm, hyhn* — **Pages 17-23**

### Overview
Mastering the diagonal vertical channels across all three letter rows. Rule for left hand: 'Up and out, Down and in'. Rule for right hand: 'Up and in, Down and out'. Always return the reaching finger immediately to its home row key.

## Lesson 2.1: Left Hand Diagonals (aqaz, swsx, dedc, frfv, gtgb)

**Instructions & Finger Movements:**
Little finger: a -> up to q -> back to a -> down to z -> space (aqaz). Ring finger: s -> up to w -> back to s -> down to x -> space (swsx). Middle finger: d -> up to e -> back to d -> down to c -> space (dedc). Index finger: f -> up to r -> back to f -> down to v -> space (frfv). Extended index: g -> up to t -> back to g -> down to b -> space (gtgb).

**Drill Practice Text:**
```text
aqaz swsx dedc frfv gtgb aqaz swsx dedc frfv gtgb aqaz swsx dedc frfv gtgb aqaz swsx dedc frfv gtgb
```

**Key Objectives:**
- Independent diagonal reaching
- Immediate home key recovery
- Relaxed hand posture
- **Mastery Benchmark:** Recite and type left diagonals without hesitation

---

## Lesson 2.2: Right Hand Diagonals (;p;/, lol., kik,, jujm, hyhn)

**Instructions & Finger Movements:**
Little finger: ; -> up to p -> back to ; -> down to / -> space (;p;/). Ring finger: l -> up to o -> back to l -> down to . -> space (lol.). Middle finger: k -> up to i -> back to k -> down to , -> space (kik,). Index finger: j -> up to u -> back to j -> down to m -> space (jujm). Extended index: h -> up to y -> back to h -> down to n -> space (hyhn).

**Drill Practice Text:**
```text
;p;/ lol. kik, jujm hyhn ;p;/ lol. kik, jujm hyhn ;p;/ lol. kik, jujm hyhn ;p;/ lol. kik, jujm hyhn
```

**Key Objectives:**
- Right diagonal coordinate mastery
- Punctuation reaches for period and comma
- Even touch
- **Mastery Benchmark:** Recite and type right diagonals without hesitation

---

## Lesson 2.3: Full Diagonal Channels & Practice Model 2

# UNIT 4 — CAPITAL LETTERS, PUNCTUATION, ABBREVIATIONS & AILMENTS
*Shift keys, periods, commas, colons, abbreviations & typing remedies* — **Pages 28-34**

### Overview
Proper technique for Shift keys (always use the opposite hand's pinky to hold Shift down while the other hand strikes the letter). Spacing rules: space once after comma or semicolon; space twice after period, colon, question mark, or exclamation point completing a sentence.

## Lesson 4.1: Opposite-Hand Shift Key Technique

**Instructions & Finger Movements:**
To capitalize a right-hand letter (J, K, L, U, I, O, P, H, N, M), hold Left Shift with left little finger. To capitalize a left-hand letter (A, S, D, F, Q, W, E, R, T, G, Z, X, C, V, B), hold Right Shift with right little finger. Hold Shift down until the stroke is fully completed.

**Drill Practice Text:**
```text
John Mary Paul Ruth Frank David Sarah Alice London Paris Rome New York Chicago Boston
```

**Key Objectives:**
- Opposite shift coordination
- Even height capitals (no flying capitals)
- Zero rhythm interruption
- **Mastery Benchmark:** 100% correct opposite-hand shift execution

---

## Lesson 4.2: Punctuation Spacing & Practice Models 4A & 4B

**Instructions & Finger Movements:**
Rule 1: After a comma or semicolon, space once. Rule 2: After a period or colon completing a sentence, space twice. Rule 3: Do not space before punctuation marks.

**Drill Practice Text:**
```text
No one is so old as to think he cannot live one more year. It is work which gives flavor to life. After a comma or a semicolon, space once. After a period or colon which completes a sentence, space twice. Would you remember to shift for the colon?
```

**Key Objectives:**
- Correct punctuation spacing
- Automated sentence capitalization
- 98%+ accuracy
- **Mastery Benchmark:** Type Practice Model 4B with zero spacing errors

---

## Lesson 4.3: Ailments and Remedies & Practice Model 4C

**Instructions & Finger Movements:**
Ailment: Flying capitals -> Remedy: Hold shift down firmly until stroke completes. Ailment: Irregular left margin -> Remedy: Return carriage or press enter smoothly without slamming. Ailment: Sluggish typing -> Remedy: Strike keys with sharp staccato snap.

**Drill Practice Text:**
```text
Posture is important in typing. Keep your feet flat on the floor and your wrists relaxed. Sharp, light, staccato strokes produce clear and clean print. Hold the shift key down firmly until the stroke has been completed.
```

**Key Objectives:**
- Master typing mechanics
- Eliminate physical errors
- Smooth carriage rhythm
- **Mastery Benchmark:** Clean, even print without flying capitals
